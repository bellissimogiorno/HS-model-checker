Language guide and interpretation
Theory documents
A fenced hsm-model block contains model JSON. Fenced hsm blocks contain declarations such as include ThyLive, axiom Echo? [backward]: ∀ v. echo(v) ⇒ ♢[] ↓ propose(v), and property Sequential [end]: seq. Other Markdown is commentary.
Logic and histories
Use ¬ ∧ ∨ ⇒ ⇔, ∀ ∃ ∃=1 ∃⩽1, ↓ ⇓ ↕ ⇕, ♢[l,m], □[l,m], end and seq. ASCII spellings such as forall, -> and dia are also accepted; the notation table above lists them. Values also serve as learners. † is JSON null.
Finite evidence
A counterexample satisfies the retained assumptions and falsifies the selected property. No model in scope means that no interpretation within those bounds satisfies the assumptions; it does not establish a property. A completed search covers only its stated finite scope. An incomplete search leaves the remaining questions open.
Full schemes, facts and privacy
Retaining ThyLive includes its complete dynamic Knowledge schemes, not a fixed list of instances. Quorum modalities still admit learner lists, and repeated learners choose quorums independently. The Knowledge□ scheme itself has one learner. The predicates live, safe and corr are ordinary interpreted predicates. corr represents dotted correlation, distinct from value equality.
A DAG stores shared histories; only node pasts and the full history are represented times. Facts may vary with place and time. JSON past references are generators of transitive closure; equal mathematical tuples merge.
Theory documents, models and results remain on your device. Browser storage keeps a local draft when available; download a project for a portable copy. Closing the page stops computation.

    

      
About HSmc

      
History Structure Model-checker 0.7.1 is a proof-of-concept for exploring protocols over finite history structures. A completed check establishes an outcome only within its stated scope.

      
This package was vibe-coded following prompts by Murdoch J. Gabbay.

      
It is based on joint work with Isaac Sheff: Heterogeneous trust in reliable broadcast via modal logic and history structures, by Murdoch J. Gabbay and Isaac Sheff. The implementation follows the reference draft of 18 November 2026; see the paper-to-code map ↗ for exact references.

      
The interface and SAT solver are adapted from 3μQL Explorer under its MIT licence. See upstream acknowledgements ↗ and the licence ↗. This experimental implementation has not received an external expert audit.
